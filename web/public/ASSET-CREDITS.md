## Card backs and book emblem
Single Leaves card backs preserve the approved mirrored composition, broad corner leaves, and paired open books, with a solid cream outer border and one fine gold inner line. The leaf and open-book shapes adapt Lucide icon geometry (ISC License, `cards/LICENSE-lucide.txt`): https://lucide.dev/

Light mode uses M3 Rose Clay. Dark mode uses the approved petal and antique-gold palette. Regular and miniature SVG variants use the same 160 × 224 coordinate space; miniature strokes are strengthened for readability.

## Interface and suit icons

Navigation, audio controls, standard suit shapes, and the shared account profile catalog reuse the same licensed Font Awesome Pro artwork as the sister sites. The interface artwork is bundled in `src/components/iconData.ts`; profile artwork loads separately from `src/lib/profileIconData.ts`. The accompanying notice is in `icons/LICENSE-fontawesome.txt`.
